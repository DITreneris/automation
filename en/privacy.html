Privacy policy

Prompt Anatomy – a minimal app. A brief note about your data.

Do we collect your data?

No account, no form. We do not collect names, emails, or form submissions. There is no contact form.

What happens on your device?

Only your browser's localStorage: we save which prompts you marked as “done”. Data stays on your device only.

Analytics

Library and privacy pages load Vercel Web Analytics. It records page path, referrer, country, and device so we can see which pages are used. No cookies and no account.

If we add a form later

If we enable a contact form or similar, this policy will be updated – we will clearly state what we collect and how we use it.
